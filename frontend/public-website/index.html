About Sri Vidya High School

Fostering excellence, ethical character, and academic mastery since 2017 in Visakhapatnam.

Foundational Heritage

Established in 2017, Sri Vidya E.M High School was founded with a singular purpose: to deliver high-calibre, affordable, English-medium schooling in Pendurthi, Visakhapatnam that balances rigorous academic learning with deep-rooted Indian values.

  • Recognition: AP State Education Department (Code: AP-EDS-2017-SVEMHS)
  • Medium of Instruction: English (with Telugu and Hindi language electives)
  • Co-educational campus spanning Pre-Primary (Nursery) to Class X (SSC Board)

Admissions 2026 - 2027

We warmly invite parents seeking value-based English medium education to enroll their children in Sri Vidya E.M High School.

1

Step 1: Enquiry & Registration

Fill out our online enquiry form or visit our admissions desk at campus to collect the prospectus and application kit.

2

Step 2: Document Verification

Submit photocopies of the birth certificate, previous school progress report, student/parent Aadhaar cards, and Transfer Certificate.

3

Step 3: Interaction & Counseling

A friendly, informal interaction with the Principal and teachers to understand the child's interests and learning style.

4

Step 4: Admission Confirmation

Fee payment, allocation of student roll number, textbook kit collection, and welcome orientation schedule.
