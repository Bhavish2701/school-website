Sri Vidya English Medium High School employs limited cookies and browser storage technologies strictly to operate the School ERP platform securely, authenticate authorized staff and parents, and maintain session continuity. In compliance with the Indian DPDP Act 2023 and GDPR Article 7, we strictly forbid third-party advertising, commercial tracking, or behavioural profiling of minor students.

Strict Prohibition on Third-Party Commercial Tracking

Under Section 9 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, data fiduciaries operating educational software for minor students must never track their online habits or target behavioral advertisements. Sri Vidya ERP embeds zero Google Ads, Meta Pixels, or external ad-tracking scripts.
