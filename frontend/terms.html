Official Institutional Policy Authorized Personnel Only Effective: 2026-2027 Academic Session

These Terms of Service & Acceptable Use Policy ("Terms") govern access to and usage of the Sri Vidya English Medium High School Management Information System and Enterprise Resource Planning Portal ("Sri Vidya ERP"). By accessing or utilizing any portal module, authorized administrators, teachers, clerks, parents, and students agree to comply with these terms.

1. Authorized Access & Credential Confidentiality

Access to the Sri Vidya ERP is strictly restricted to authenticated individuals with explicitly assigned institutional credentials. Sharing login passwords, 2FA codes, or session tokens with unauthorized persons is strictly prohibited and constitutes an institutional disciplinary violation.

  • Users must immediately notify the Principal's Office or IT Administrator upon detecting any suspected compromise of their credentials.
  • Sessions idle for greater than 30 minutes will be automatically terminated for security safeguarding.

2. Academic Integrity & Marksheet Immutability

Faculty members are granted authorization to enter scholastic evaluation scores and daily attendance for their assigned classes. Any intentional falsification of academic evaluation scores, retroactive attendance manipulation, or unauthorized alteration of fee concession records is strictly prohibited.

All grade mutations, attendance lock overrides, and fee adjustments are permanently captured in the immutable Sri Vidya ERP Audit Trail with the responsible operator's identity and timestamp.

3. Fee Ledgers & Institutional Receipts

Official school receipts issued by Sri Vidya ERP are legal documents representing payment against authorized school fee heads. Concessions or waivers require executive digital authorization by the Principal or School Secretary before application to student ledgers.

4. System Security & Rate Limiting

Users must not attempt to circumvent role-based access boundaries, probe API endpoints with automated scripts, inject malicious SQL/NoSQL payloads, or tamper with institutional backups. Rate limits are programmatically enforced across authentication and reporting endpoints.
