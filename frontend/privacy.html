Sri Vidya English Medium High School ("Institution", "we", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and data integrity of our students, guardians, faculty, and administrative staff. This Policy outlines how personal and academic data is collected, processed, and safeguarded within the Sri Vidya Unified School Management ERP in strict accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act 2023) and statutory directives of the Andhra Pradesh Board of Secondary Education.

1. Categories of Data Collected

We collect and process only data strictly necessary for educational, administrative, and statutory governance:

  • Student Demographic & Academic Data: Full legal name, date of birth, Aadhaar/ID number (stored with role masking), admission number, enrolled class, section, attendance logs, scholastic marks, progress reports, and Transfer Certificate records.
  • Parent & Guardian Information: Guardian legal names, authorized contact numbers, residential address, emergency contact coordinates, and fee payment ledger histories.
  • Faculty & Staff Records: Teacher credentials, payroll rosters, qualifications, teaching assignments, and institutional attendance logs.
  • System Telemetry & Audit Logs: Access logs, session correlation IDs, and immutable audit trails of all sensitive record mutations (grade alterations, fee concessions, TC issuances).

2. Minor Student Protections (DPDP Act 2023)

In strict adherence to Section 9 of the DPDP Act 2023, Sri Vidya ERP does NOT engage in behavioral profiling, targeted commercial advertising, or third-party data tracking of minor students.

All student personal data is processed strictly under verified parental or legal guardian consent acquired during the formal admission process. Third-party analytical tools, tracking pixels, and external advertising scripts are strictly prohibited across all Sri Vidya portals.

3. Data Security & Access Controls

We deploy robust enterprise safeguards to defend all institutional data against unauthorized access:

  • Cryptographic Encryption: 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit; one-way salted bcrypt hashing for passwords; AES-256 for database snapshots and backups.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Strict separation between Super Admin, Principal, Vice Principal, Faculty, Office Cashiers, and Parents. Faculty access is dynamically locked to assigned classes and subjects.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Two-factor verification codes for administrative and executive logins.

4. Statutory Data Retention & Archival

Under Andhra Pradesh Education Department rules, certain scholastic records (such as Permanent Register entries, Mark Registers, and Transfer Certificate counterfoils) are statutory legal instruments that must be preserved for minimum legally mandated retention periods (up to 7 years or permanently). When an account deletion is requested, personal contact details may be anonymized, but official academic history remains safeguarded in compliance with state regulations.

5. Data Subject Rights & Grievance Redressal

Guardians and authorized staff possess the right to review their recorded personal data, request correction of inaccurate records, or seek account deactivation through the authorized Principal Office approval workflow.

6. Lawful Grounds for Data Processing (DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR Art 6)

Personal data is collected and processed strictly under established statutory and legal grounds:

  • Contractual & Educational Obligation: Delivering curriculum, administering examinations, tabulating results, and issuing scholastic certificates (DPDP Act Sec 4, GDPR Art 6(1)(b)).
  • Legal & Statutory Compliance: Preserving mandatory admission registers, fee ledgers, and TC counterfoils under the AP Education Act 1982 Section 17 and Income Tax Act Section 44AA (GDPR Art 6(1)(c)).
  • Explicit & Parental Consent: Processing minor student records with verified parental consent, SMS attendance alerts, and WhatsApp circular dispatches (DPDP Act Sec 6 & Sec 9).
  • Vital Interests & Campus Safety: Maintaining gate logs, medical emergency contacts, and disaster contingency registers (GDPR Art 6(1)(d)).
