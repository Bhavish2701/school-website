Institutional Fee Structure & Refund Policy

Return to Login

This institutional charter sets forth the official fee schedules, payment modes, receipt validation rules, and statutory refund/cancellation guidelines for Sri Vidya English Medium High School in accordance with the Andhra Pradesh Education Act, 1982 and governing Department of School Education directives.

1. Prescribed Fee Heads & Schedules

School fees are structured across four authorized heads: (a) Tuition Fee, (b) Laboratory & Computer Science Fee, (c) Examination & Scholastic Assessment Fee, and (d) Sports & Extracurricular Activity Fee. Fees are payable in three term installments (June, October, and January) or via annual single payment with authorized concession.

2. Official Digital Receipts & Ledger Verification

All fee payments must be acknowledged by an official machine-generated Sri Vidya ERP Receipt bearing a unique receipt docket reference (e.g. `REC-2026-XXXX`). Hand-written receipts without ERP synchronization are strictly non-authoritative.

3. Withdrawal, Cancellation & Refund Rules

  • Withdrawal before term commencement: If a student withdrawal is requested before the formal commencement of the academic term, tuition fees paid in advance will be refunded after deduction of a statutory administrative processing fee not exceeding £500 / ₹1,000 as regulated by state educational orders.
  • Mid-Term Transfers: Where a Transfer Certificate (TC) is requested mid-term due to parent job relocation, fees for subsequent uncommenced terms will not be levied. Term fees for the active instructional term are non-refundable.
  • Statutory Preservation: In accordance with statutory auditing guidelines, all financial ledgers, waivers, concessions, and canceled receipts are permanently archived in the ERP ledger for minimum 8 statutory assessment years.

4. Concessions & Financial Aid

Merit scholarships and sibling fee concessions require verified application through the Principal Office. Concessions approved by Principal Smt. Gorle Varalakshmi are digitally locked to the student fee ledger and cannot be overridden by office staff.
